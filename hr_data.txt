LEAVE POLICY
Employees get 18 days paid annual leave per year.
Leave must be applied 3 days in advance via HR portal.
Unused leave can carry forward up to 5 days.
Sick leave is 10 days per year.
Maternity leave is 26 weeks. Paternity leave is 5 days.

SALARY AND PAYROLL
Salaries are credited on the last working day of every month.
Salary increments happen every April based on performance.
Performance reviews happen every March.
Bonuses are paid in first week of April.

OFFICE TIMINGS
Office hours are 9am to 6pm Monday to Friday.
Work from home is allowed up to 2 days per week.
The office is in Koramangala, Bengaluru.

BENEFITS
Health insurance covers employee spouse and 2 children.
Insurance limit is 5 lakhs per family per year.
Gym reimbursement is Rs 2000 per month.
Internet reimbursement is Rs 1000 per month for WFH.

JOINING AND EXIT
Notice period is 60 days for all employees.
Full and final settlement is within 45 days of exit.
Referral bonus is Rs 25000 after 3 months of joining.
